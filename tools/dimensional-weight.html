Calculation method

This worksheet uses the following planning method:

Dimensional weight = length × width × height ÷ DIM divisor

Worked example

A 12 × 10 × 8 in carton has 960 in³ of volume. Using a 139 divisor, its DIM weight is 6.91 lb, commonly rounded up to 7 lb for rating.

How to interpret the result

Compare the rounded DIM weight with the scale weight. A carrier may bill whichever is greater, subject to its current service rules.

Assumptions and limitations

The calculator does not select a divisor for you or quote a shipment. Confirm the divisor, rounding method, and minimums with the carrier or marketplace you use.
