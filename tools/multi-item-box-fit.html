Calculation method

This calculator uses the following planning method:

Grid capacity = maximum of floor(box side ÷ rotated item side) products

Worked example

A 7 × 5 × 3 in item is checked in six orthogonal orientations inside a 16 × 12 × 10 in box.

How to interpret the result

Use the result as an operating plan, compare it with the relevant capacity or budget, and record the assumptions used for the batch.

Assumptions and limitations

This is an orthogonal grid estimate, not a complete 3D bin-packing optimizer. It excludes nesting, irregular shapes, cushioning, loading sequence, compression, and orientation restrictions.

Estimate only. Verify the packed result with actual materials and the current requirements of your supplier, marketplace, or carrier.
