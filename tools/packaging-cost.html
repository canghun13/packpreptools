Calculation method

This calculator uses the following planning method:

Cost per order = materials × (1 + waste %) + labor minutes ÷ 60 × hourly rate

Worked example

Materials totaling $1.42 with 5% waste become $1.49. Four minutes at $18/hour adds $1.20, for about $2.69 per order.

How to interpret the result

Use the total as an internal packing-cost baseline. Compare it by SKU, pack method, or month to find repeatable savings.

Assumptions and limitations

Postage, product cost, marketplace fees, rent, equipment depreciation, and taxes are excluded unless entered as other materials.

Estimate only. Verify the packed result with actual materials and the current requirements of your supplier, marketplace, or carrier.
