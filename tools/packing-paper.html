Calculation method

This calculator uses the following planning method:

Sheets = round up [void volume ÷ (sheet length × sheet width × tested yield depth)]

Worked example

An 880 in³ void divided by an estimated 864 in³ crumpled yield per 24 × 18 in sheet rounds up to 2 sheets.

How to interpret the result

Calibrate yield depth by packing several real orders with your paper weight and operator method.

Assumptions and limitations

Paper does not fill space like a solid block. This planning estimate must be calibrated with repeatable bench tests.

Estimate only. Verify the packed result with actual materials and the current requirements of your supplier, marketplace, or carrier.
