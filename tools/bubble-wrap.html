Calculation method

This worksheet uses the following planning method:

Wrap area = product surface area × layers × (1 + overlap percentage)

Worked example

A 10 × 6 × 4 in product has 248 in² of surface. Two layers plus 15% overlap require about 570.4 in², or 3.96 ft².

How to interpret the result

Divide the required area by your roll width to estimate a cut length, then round up for handling and corner coverage.

Assumptions and limitations

The product is modeled as a rectangular prism. Seams, corner bunching, fragile projections, and operator technique can increase use.

Estimate only. Verify the packed result with actual materials and the current requirements of your supplier, marketplace, or carrier.
