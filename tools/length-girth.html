Calculation method

This worksheet uses the following planning method:

Length + girth = longest side + 2 × (second side + shortest side)

Worked example

For a 30 × 18 × 12 in carton, the longest side is 30 in and girth is 60 in. Length plus girth is 90 in.

How to interpret the result

Use the result to compare the package with a published carrier size limit. Carrier definitions and limits can change.

Assumptions and limitations

The longest dimension is treated as length automatically. Bulges and irregular shapes must be measured at their widest points.

Estimate only. Verify the packed result with actual materials and the current requirements of your supplier, marketplace, or carrier.
