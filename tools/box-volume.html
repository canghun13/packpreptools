Calculation method

This worksheet uses the following planning method:

Box volume = internal length × internal width × internal height

Worked example

A 12 × 10 × 8 in box has 960 in³ of internal volume, equal to about 15.73 L or 0.56 ft³.

How to interpret the result

Internal volume helps compare cartons and estimate unused space. It does not by itself prove that a product shape will fit.

Assumptions and limitations

The box is treated as a perfect rectangular prism. Use internal dimensions when estimating usable capacity.
