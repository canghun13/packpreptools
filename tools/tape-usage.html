Calculation method

This worksheet uses the following planning method:

Center seam = 2 × (length + 2 × overhang); H-seal adds 4 × (width + 2 × overhang)

Worked example

For 100 cartons measuring 14 × 10 in with 2 in overhang, a center seam uses 3,600 in, or 300 ft, of tape.

How to interpret the result

Compare the total with roll length and add a shop-specific setup and waste allowance before ordering.

Assumptions and limitations

The model covers top and bottom seals. Reinforcement, poor adhesion, rework, tabs, and dispenser losses are excluded.

Estimate only. Verify the packed result with actual materials and the current requirements of your supplier, marketplace, or carrier.
