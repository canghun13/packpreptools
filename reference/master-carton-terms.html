Master Carton Terms

Use the same master-carton terms across purchasing, packing, storage, and pallet planning.

On this page
  • Definitions and operating notes
  • Verification

Definitions and operating notes

Packed unit
The finished inner item or retail pack placed into the master carton.
Case pack
The standard number of saleable units assigned to a sealed case or carton.
Columns, rows, layers
The explicit orthogonal arrangement used for the dimension estimate.
Internal dimensions
Usable carton space for the planned layout, gaps, dividers, and clearance.
External dimensions
Finished outside dimensions used for cube, storage, and unit-load planning.
Tare weight
Carton, dividers, liners, closure, and other packing weight without product.
Gross packed weight
Product weight plus tare weight for the completed carton.
Carton cube
External length × width × height, stated in a consistent cubic unit.
Reference note. Confirm current supplier and carrier specifications before using a term or value operationally.
