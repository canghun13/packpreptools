How to Choose Void Fill

Void fill should control movement without pretending to provide structural support it cannot deliver.

Prepare the operating record

  1. Define whether the material must block movement, wrap surfaces, brace weight, or only occupy headspace.
  2. Calculate approximate void using internal box and protected-product dimensions.
  3. Collect supplier instructions and create a small calibration batch.
  4. Record material quantity, dispensing setting, pack time, movement, and closure result.

Define the job

Separate blocking, bracing, wrapping, surface protection, and presentation. Loose fill may occupy space while a heavy or fragile product still needs engineered support.

In How to Choose Void Fill, document the define the job choice, its measured basis, and any exception that changes the standard procedure.

Compare materials

Paper, air pillows, foam systems, and other fills differ in recovery, compression, dust, storage, equipment, and operator technique. Use supplier data as a starting point.

In How to Choose Void Fill, document the compare materials choice, its measured basis, and any exception that changes the standard procedure.

Calibrate yield

Pack a measured sample, record the dispensing or sheet quantity, and divide output by filled void. Use a conservative adjustable factor in planning.

In How to Choose Void Fill, document the calibrate yield choice, its measured basis, and any exception that changes the standard procedure.

Verify the pack

Check movement, corners, closure, compression after storage, and final dimensions. Repeat after material, equipment, carton, or operator changes.

In How to Choose Void Fill, document the verify the pack choice, its measured basis, and any exception that changes the standard procedure.

Working scenario

A 14 × 10 × 8 in carton around a protected 10 × 6 × 4 in item has substantial geometric void. Paper may be suitable when folded and crumpled to block movement; air pillows may fill upper space quickly but may not brace a dense item. The choice is made from the protection job, measured yield, storage, and handling outcome—not from liters of void alone.

Decision guide

ObservationOperational response
Heavy product shiftsUse designed blocking or bracing; loose fill volume is not enough.
Large upper voidEvaluate lightweight fill while confirming compression recovery and closure.
Surface is sensitiveSeparate wrapping protection from space filling.
Yield varies widelyStandardize method and recalibrate before setting inventory demand.

Common mistakes

  • Calling every cushioning material interchangeable void fill.
  • Using supplier yield as guaranteed production yield.
  • Filling space while leaving the product free to move.
  • Changing material without updating quantity and work instructions.

Complete and verify the work

Close How to Choose Void Fill only after its physical result, controlled instruction, and recorded measurements agree with the decision criteria above.

  • Protection function is named.
  • Actual yield is documented.
  • Movement and closure checks pass.
  • Inventory rate matches the approved method.

Evidence, ownership, and review triggers

The How to Choose Void Fill record should connect the initial requirement—define whether the material must block movement, wrap surfaces, brace weight, or only occupy headspace—to the released evidence that protection function is named. Keep the How to Choose Void Fill inputs, sample identification, material or equipment revision, date, operator or reviewer role, and exception decision together so a later result can be compared on the same basis.

Reopen How to Choose Void Fill when calling every cushioning material interchangeable void fill is observed, when the product or packaging specification changes, or when damage, rework, time, or consumption moves outside the accepted range. The How to Choose Void Fill owner should compare the new condition with the working scenario, repeat the relevant physical check, and issue a revised instruction rather than silently changing an input.

For periodic review, sample normal work as well as known exceptions. Confirm that the response to “Heavy product shifts” still follows the recorded action: Use designed blocking or bracing; loose fill volume is not enough. Retain the How to Choose Void Fill evidence long enough to explain inventory settings, cost changes, and any customer or carrier inquiry tied to the pack method.

Dispatch checklist

  • Calculate the approximate void.
  • Identify protection function.
  • Measure real yield.
  • Document the approved quantity range.
How to Choose Void Fill planning note: validate the How to Choose Void Fill method with the actual product, materials, handling path, and current shipping requirements.
