Flexible Packaging Film & Seal Test Tools

Film tests / 04 decisions

Start with the reading you have

Use friction readings to compare surface behavior, tensile readings to compare intact strips, dart outcomes to review impact response, and peel readings to review a finished seal.

  1. FT01Film Coefficient of Friction CalculatorMean static and kinetic COF, ranges, and replicate checks
  2. FT02Film Tensile Strength & Elongation CalculatorPeak stress and elongation at break for each specimen
  3. FT03Film Dart Impact Staircase CalculatorSequence validation, estimated failure mass, and mass-level tally
  4. FT04Flexible Package Seal Strength CalculatorWidth-normalized peel peaks, range, and below-threshold count

Keep the conditions with the result

These tools interpret measurements, not package performance or standards compliance.

  1. 01 / RecordKeep the method, material lot, direction and conditioning with the source readings.
  2. 02 / ReviewCheck individual results before relying on a mean or comparing batches.
  3. 03 / Follow upUse packaging trial and damage review for pack-level evidence.
