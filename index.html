Bench sequence

A calculator is one step in a better pack.

Use measured inputs, treat the result as a planning specification, and verify it with production materials.

01 / MEASURE

Capture the finished dimensions.

Know whether the task needs internal fit dimensions or external shipping dimensions.

02 / CALCULATE

Record the assumptions.

Choose the correct unit, divisor, clearance, material yield, or labor rate.

03 / VERIFY

Run a physical pack test.

Confirm fit, protection, closure, movement, and the final package size at the bench.
