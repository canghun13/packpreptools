## Shortlist and competition evidence

- **Film/seal readings:** 4 independent tasks after excluding Elmendorf as a weaker fifth, hot tack as a separate method, and laminate bond peel as too close to the peel engine. Same buyer/lab receives readings at each new lot, film direction, package setup or supplier qualification. No web API, stored inputs, instrument integration, standards certification or lab accreditation is needed to deliver the measured-data outputs.
- **Closure setup:** [SGSBOTTLE's finder](https://www.sgsbottle.com/resources/specs-and-compatibility/neck-finish-finder/) matches by finish; [Enercon's line-speed tool](https://www.enerconind.com/sealing/library/line-speed-calculator/) already computes line speed/dwell relationships; [Silgan's technical guidance](https://www.silgancls.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Silgan-Closures-TAB14-Plastic-Closure-Quality-Checks-Rev-C.pdf) points to closure-specific process and torque evaluation. Useful measured-torque review remains, but not four clear independent gaps.
- **Barrier and integrity:** [Packaging HQ's converter](https://packaginghq.com/resources/barrier-unit-converter) covers OTR, WVTR and conditions context without providing shelf-life guarantees. [Zaxis](https://zaxisinc.com/resources/what-is-a-leak-test/leak-rate-calculator.html) already exposes pressure drop, enclosed volume and elapsed time, with sccm and pressure-volume conversion; [LeakMaster](https://www.leakmasterusa.com/tools/leak-rate-calculator) covers pressure-decay timing. Burst results are not leak or peel results, but remain fixture-dependent and vendor-led. Do not turn four measurement units into pages.
- **Board lab and sourcing:** [FEFCO's production example](https://www.fefco.org/node/1588) and [NR Tech Studio's board calculator](https://nrtechstudio.com/tools/corrugated-board-weight-calculator/) cover liner/medium and adjustable flute take-up; [Packaging Squad](https://packagingsquad.com/tools/paper-weight-converter/) has packaging grades and lb/MSF conversion. Cobb has a concrete readout, but board weight and caliper have exact direct alternatives, and moisture/absorption/gas flow are not an equally coherent four-gap cluster.
- **Transit data:** [KasperCalc](https://kaspercalc.com/RandomVibrationPSD.html) already calculates GRMS from PSD, [MegaRows](https://megarows.com/psd-calculator) computes PSD from CSV, and [Hamfy](https://hamfy.eu/en/tools/sensor-analysis) demonstrates threshold/series analysis for sensors. Package pressure and shock interpretations could be genuinely useful in a narrow niche, but validated pressure, sensor orientation and exact/free competition leave fewer than four strong standalone tools. No revival of the old cold-chain planning candidate.

### Final four — Tool-specific checks

| Tool / query set | User intent and observed SERP | Static advantage and separate action |
|---|---|---|
| **Film COF** — `film COF calculation static kinetic sled`, `coefficient of friction calculator film sled ASTM D1894` | User has static peak and kinetic average forces, not an angle-of-repose problem. [Intertek](https://www.intertek.com/polymers-plastics/testlopedia/coefficient-of-friction-astm-d1894/) explains the film test and reports the two coefficients; [UD Packaging](https://plastics.udpackaging.com/blog/astm-d1894/) discusses interpreting film readings. Test-service and fixture results dominate the checked phrases. | Sled mass + paired N/lbf/gf values → separate ratios and replicate ranges → decide which surface readings to recheck. No film selection or machine recommendation. |
| **Film Tensile Strength & Elongation** — `ASTM D882 film tensile strength calculate cross section elongation`, `tensile strength elongation calculator plastic film strip sample` | User wants measured force reported on the original film section, and stretch at a different event. [ADMET](https://www.admet.com/testing-standards/astm-d882-thin-plastic-film-tensile-testing/) and [ZwickRoell](https://www.zwickroell.com/industries/plastics/thin-sheeting-and-thin-plastic-films/astm-d882-film-tensile-test/) are equipment/method explanations. [ToolsRail](https://www.toolsrail.com/mechanical-engineering/stress-strain-calculator.php) is a free generic stress/strain solver, with area as a separate input, not a film batch worksheet. | Width + actual micron thickness + initial gauge length + peak force + extension at break → per-strip MPa and % plus ranges → compare repeat results without equating peak and break. |
| **Film Dart Impact Staircase** — `dart impact failure weight staircase calculator`, `film dart impact test calculate F50 staircase spreadsheet` | User wants to reduce ordered pass/fail records, not calculate a shipping drop height. [Intertek](https://www.intertek.com/polymers-plastics/testlopedia/drop-dart-method-astm-d1709/) explains the measured quantity; [Testing Machines' manual](https://assets-cdn-prod-dfauhca6edaxdvgy.a01.azurefd.net/assets%2FMultimedia%2FBYK_Instruments%2FBYK%20Instruments%20MAM%2F04_Manuals%2F01-Current%2F03-Physical-Properties%2F11_IMPACT_FLEXIBILITY%2FImpact-Tester%2FFalling-Dart-Impact-Tester_2016%2FManual_TMI_Falling-Dart-Impact-Tester.pdf) documents instrument analysis. Supplier formulas in results were not uniform, so the worksheet is explicitly exploratory and uses a [NBS up-and-down estimator](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/hb/nbshandbook91.pdf), not a named standards report. | Total mass, fixed increment, and chronological pass/fail → sequence validity, mass tally and 50% point estimate → reconcile against instrument data; no sample selection, bounds or disposition. |
| **Flexible Package Seal Strength** — `seal strength N/15mm lbf/in calculator`, `ASTM F88 seal strength peak force per width calculate` | User has strip widths and measured peel peaks. [ADMET](https://www.admet.com/testing-standards/astm-f88-package-seal-strength-testing/) distinguishes peak per width, failure mode and mean peel; [PennPac](https://www.pennpac.com/films/calculators/) already provides single-value seal unit conversions. Compete on the review workflow, not on conversion alone. | Width + observed peak per strip → normalized N/15 mm/lbf/in and optional below-threshold count → inspect individual low strips without declaring pass/fail or leak status. |

Long-tail branches are static/kinetic COF and film surfaces; film strip thickness, tensile MPa and elongation at break; falling-dart mass sequence and F50; seal strip width, peak force and low-strip review. The strongest differentiation is Dart and COF; tensile and seal have direct partial alternatives and compete through film-specific, multi-specimen inputs. Demand signals are recurring instrument/test documentation, search results with manual calculations, and repeated specimen/batch tasks; no GSC query for these pages, exact search volumes, rankings or incremental traffic were established.
